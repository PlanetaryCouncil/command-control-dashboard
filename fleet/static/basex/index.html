BaseX

a new definition of value — triple bottom line (people, planet, prosperity)

Status: awaiting deployment — 27 August 2026. The domain is registered; this page is the first thing a visitor can actually read.

People

BaseX counts people first because a unit of value that cannot name a life it improved is not a unit of value. What is counted is who is served, who is harmed, who is asked, and who is left out. A project, product, or policy scores on whether the people it touches can live, work, and decide with more dignity than before — not on whether they were reached. Named humans and communities, not abstracted users. Consent and the right to leave are first-class. Unpaid care, safety, and time returned to people are assets, not residuals. If a gain in another column is paid for by someone else's hours, health, or voice, that cost is recorded here and not netted away. People is not a marketing pillar. It is the ledger of who the definition of value is for.

Planet

BaseX counts the planet as a bottom line because a surplus that depletes the ground it stands on is a delay, not a profit. What is counted: energy, materials, land, water, living systems, and the waste and risk left after a use. Every claim of value must say what was drawn down, what was restored, and over what horizon the remainder is someone else's problem. Carbon is one line, not the only line. Extractive throughput that looks cheap because the sink is unpriced fails this column. Regenerative work that keeps soil, habitat, and climate within bounds succeeds even when it is slower. Planet is not an offset against prosperity. It is the condition that makes the other two columns possible next year, and the year after that.

Prosperity

BaseX counts prosperity as the capacity to keep going — livelihoods, tools, surplus that can be shared — without converting people or planet into the input. What is counted is whether the activity produces enough to pay its workers, maintain its tools, and leave a remainder that can be reinvested in the other two columns. Money is a meter, not the definition. A cash profit that required unpaid harm in People or an uncounted drawdown in Planet is not prosperity; it is a transfer this ledger refuses to call a gain. Prosperity includes time, skill, and durable infrastructure. It excludes extraction dressed as growth. The three columns are not weighted after the fact. A result is valuable only when it can be stated in all three without a remainder that has to be hidden.

Architecture

An activity is recorded on three ledgers at once. Nothing is netted across them. The impact score is the geometric mean of People, Planet, and Prosperity, so a zero in any column is a zero overall.

Impact score

Founders and project stewards enter three metrics from 0 to 100: community (People), ecological (Planet), and financial (Prosperity). The BaseX impact score is their geometric mean, rounded. Examples: 100, 100, 100 → 100. 8, 27, 64 → 24. 0, 100, 100 → 0.
