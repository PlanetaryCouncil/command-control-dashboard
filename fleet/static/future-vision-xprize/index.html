What an imagination athlete is

An imagination athlete trains the capacity to see a future that is not yet granted as possible, and to write it so other people can stand in it.

They treat a vision as a practice: make it more ambitious, throw away the version that is merely likely, and keep going until the picture is large enough to be worth arguing with.

The competition is not who can invent a gadget; it is who can enlarge what a serious person is allowed to believe is buildable.

What the prize is for

This prize is not a grant for a device. It exists to expand the horizon of what people believe is buildable — to move the line of what counts as a serious proposal, not to fund the next prototype in a known category.

An entry that describes hardware and a budget has missed the point. An entry that makes a previously unthinkable future feel addressable has not.
