Future Vision XPRIZE

Imagination athlete — expanding the horizons of the imaginable.

What it is

Imagination athlete — expanding the horizons of the imaginable. An imagination athlete trains seeing a future not yet granted as possible, and writing it so others can stand in it. This prize is not a grant for a device. It expands the horizon of the buildable, not the next known prototype.

Where it lives

The live competition is at futurevisionxprize.com. This page exists so the project list has somewhere to send a reader. Entries, the vote, and the prize rules stay there; they are not duplicated here.

What it has published

Submissions closed after 2,500 films. Fifty remain. The public vote is open at vote.futurevisionxprize.com. Finals are judged live at Moonshots LIVE on 25 September 2026 in Los Angeles. Until those sources change, they are the source, not this page.

Marsita's film for the prize is New Hope, Permission Not Required?. Watch it at youtube.com/watch?v=mjeihsU0M-8.

How to take part

Start with New Hope, Permission Not Required?.

Watch the top 50 and vote at the Specs Audience Choice Award. Attend the finals through moonshots.com. There is no form on this page. Nothing left to submit.
