Network State Genesis

A new model of a state with aligned values.

What it is

A new model of a state with aligned values. Citizenship is issued at the live site; a stated benefit is membership of the Planetary Council. It is not a second council, and it does not replace a government that already has reach.

Where it lives

The live site is genesis.re. This page exists so the project list has somewhere to send a reader. The digital passport, its price, and any payment stay there; they are not duplicated here.

What it has published

A founding document from 2021 and a V2 update from 2024. Technical notes sit on the BaseX Notion page. Until those documents change, they are the source, not this page.

How to take part

Citizenship and the digital passport are issued at genesis.re. The physical passport is not shipping yet. Talk to the project at @genesisdotre on X, or book Calendly. There is no form on this page.
