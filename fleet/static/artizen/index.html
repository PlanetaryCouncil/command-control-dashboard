Art funded on Artizen.

What it is

Art funded on Artizen. Four named members raise on the live platform at artizen.fund. This page is the funding process and the impact of a donation, written for those members, because the platform does not put that in one place.

How funding works

A season is a run of weekly Fund Drives. A creator submits a project to Funds; a Fund that curates it puts match behind it. Supporters buy Artifacts. Every dollar of Artifact sales, and every dollar of sponsorship, unlocks match from the Funds backing the project and from the Artizen Endowment, at that week's Match Multiple, until the available match runs out. A 10% fee on sales and sponsorships feeds the Endowment. Payout waits on an Impact Report and is due within 60 days.

What a donation does

A dollar spent on an Artifact of one of these four projects goes to that project. While match lasts, it also unlocks a dollar of match from each Fund that curated it. Rank in a Fund can win that Fund's cash prize; rank across the season can win the Grand Prize. Live amounts stay on Artizen. They are not invented here.

Who raises
