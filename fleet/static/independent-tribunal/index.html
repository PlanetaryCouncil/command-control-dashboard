Independent Tribunal

A body neither party appoints, for disputes existing authorities cannot fairly hear.

What it is

A body neither party appoints, for disputes existing authorities cannot fairly hear. It sits when the usual judge is a party to the question, or when those authorities disagree with each other. It is not a state court, and a ruling here does not displace a court that actually has reach.

What it rules on

It will hear contested designations — cases where states disagree about who a party is — and disputes where the authority that would normally decide is itself a party. It will not hear criminal matters that belong to a court with jurisdiction, private commercial fights that already have a forum, or any request to lift this site's hard block on illegal content. Until a panel is seated, nothing has been ruled.

Who sits on it

Being convened. No sitting panel is named here because none has been named. This sentence is the whole answer until that changes.

How to bring something to it

There is no form and no docket. Bring a case by writing to @IndependentTrib on X or @IndependentTribunal on Telegram, naming the dispute, the parties, and what you want heard. Those two accounts are the intake path. Nothing else on this page is.
